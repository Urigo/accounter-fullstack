The ledger record dialogs (add and edit) can switch between English and Hebrew.

- A small button in the dialog's top corner, beside the close (×), swaps the language. The choice
  is remembered in the browser and applies to every ledger record dialog.
- In Hebrew the dialog is laid out right to left: the header, the fields, the debit and credit lines,
  the totals and the submit button are mirrored, as are the operation, currency and entity pickers
  opened from it.
- Labels, placeholders, accessible names and validation messages are translated. Messages already
  shown are translated when the language is switched.
- The dialog close (×) button now sits at the dialog's end corner, which is unchanged in
  left-to-right dialogs.
