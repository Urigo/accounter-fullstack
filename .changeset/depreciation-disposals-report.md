Depreciation report: disposal columns (disposed cost and depreciation, proceeds, gain/loss) and a
"Disposals During the Year" table for sales and write-offs; cost refunds shown as a cost correction,
with refunded-cost and depreciation-correction columns and a "Cost Refunds During the Year" table;
closing figures net of both; fully depreciated assets stay on the report until they are disposed of,
and assets in categories that are not depreciated (0%) are left out.
