The edit-document sheet now shows the scan beside the form. The sheet is taller (90% of the
screen), with the form on the left and the document viewer on the right, open at Fit width with no
extra click. Each pane scrolls on its own, and Save stays in view at the bottom of the form. Drag
the handle between the panes, or use the arrow keys on it, to change the split; double-click or
Enter resets it to half and half. Hide document collapses the viewer to a slim strip and spreads the
form to four columns; Show document brings it back. Expand widens the viewer over the form for a
close read. Below 1024px the document sits above the form. Drags on the scan pan it and never move
the sheet. The thumbnail and its separate drawer are gone, and a docked viewer open on the page
hides while the sheet is open and returns when it closes.
