A Securities card in the admin business page's Admin tab shows the tenant's whole securities ledger
configuration in one place: the foreign-securities business, the sort code new securities tax
categories are created under, and the tax category of every securities key. A business owner or
accountant can assign, change or clear each setting, or create a missing tax category from its row
through the quick-fix dialog. Save sends only the changed fields, and a server-side validation
error is shown in the error toast. The card is hidden from other roles and on other businesses'
pages.
