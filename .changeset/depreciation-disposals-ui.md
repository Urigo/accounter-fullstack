Record asset disposals and cost refunds from the client: a "Dispose / refund" action on each
depreciation record and a "Record asset disposal / refund" charge action. A sale or write-off shows a
live preview of accumulated depreciation, net book value and gain or loss. A supplier cost refund is
a cost correction: it takes a refund amount and a mode ("From activation", the default, or "From
refund date"), has no proceeds, and previews the cost reduction, the depreciation correction, and
the new cost and book value. The asset and the sale/refund charge list disposals and cost refunds
(with their mode and correction) separately. A depreciation settings card sets the depreciation,
disposal gain/loss and depreciation correction (ordinary income) tax categories, and 0% categories
are labelled "not depreciated".
