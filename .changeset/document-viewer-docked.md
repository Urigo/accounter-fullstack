Document previews now open in a column docked to the right of the page instead of a drawer that
covers it, so you can read a scan and work in the table at the same time. The column takes half the
space beside the sidebar (never less than 420px, always leaving the page at least 480px), and you can
resize it by dragging or with the arrow keys on its left edge; double-click or Enter resets it.
Expand widens it over the page for a close read. Opening another document swaps it in place and
highlights its row; clicking the active thumbnail again, pressing Esc inside the viewer, or moving to
another page closes it. On narrower screens, and from tables inside dialogs, the wide drawer is used
as before.
