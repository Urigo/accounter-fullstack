Annual audit: separate Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss templates through the workflow. Step 05
shows last year's final templates as the baseline and lets you pick (and keep changing) this year's
working templates, which its report links open. Step 09 locks a final template per report, offering
the working ones first. Years locked with a single template keep using it for both. The dynamic
report now preselects a prior snapshot of the same shape as the "Compare to" baseline.
